Receivables Formulas

Outstanding invoice balance

Invoiced amount − cash received − TDS − write-offs. TDS and write-offs settle the invoice balance; they are not cash collections. Manager and panel balances sum all their filtered invoices.

Daily collections

Patient receipts + B2B invoice payments. Only the seven captured dates, 11–17 Nov 2026, have collection values. A range extending outside these dates is unavailable rather than partially summed.

Ageing

Balances are as of 17 Nov 2026 and include older open invoices. The From date controls collections, not the ageing snapshot. The four buckets and their figures are preserved as printed. Sample invoice age is days from the invoice date to the snapshot date; the printed 90+ bucket uses sample ages greater than 90 days.

Collection target and difference

Sample period target = sample November monthly collection target × captured days ÷ 30. A client or branch selection allocates that target using its share of the manager's outstanding balance. Difference = collections in the selected period − period target. Negative is Shortfall; positive is Excess; zero is Target met. These collection targets are separate from revenue and test-count targets.

Source and samples

The screenshots contain ageing totals and daily collection totals, but no manager, client or invoice identities. The missing detail is a sample ledger allocated to reconcile exactly to the printed ageing totals. Branch/client filters and drill-down details are sample allocations. They are not live debtor records. No data is written to the shared target store.

Table totals and exports

Summary values and table totals include all filtered rows across every page. Exports include the full filtered table, its date scope, manager/client context and data-source label. Panel counts are distinct client panels; open invoices exclude settled invoices.
