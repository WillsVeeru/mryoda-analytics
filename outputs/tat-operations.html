TAT & Operations Formulas

Transit & receive

Lab received timestamp − Collection timestamp

Includes transport and receipt delays. Average only records with valid timestamps.

Lab testing

Result-entry timestamp − Lab received timestamp

Measures time spent between receipt and result entry.

Doctor approval

Approval timestamp − Result-entry timestamp

Measures the approval interval after results are entered.

Total turnaround time

Approval timestamp − Collection timestamp

End-to-end time in hours. Stage means may use different eligible records; do not infer the overall mean by adding averages.

Average turnaround

Sum of eligible test turnaround hours ÷ Eligible test count

Exclude missing or invalid timestamp pairs. Category averages cannot be combined without their eligible denominators.

TAT distribution share

Tests in elapsed-time bucket ÷ Tests with valid collection-to-approval times × 100

The distribution and approval-stage counts may have different eligibility rules. Displayed rounded shares can differ from 100%.

Within 24h / 48h

Eligible tests completed within the threshold ÷ Eligible completed tests × 100

These are elapsed-time thresholds, not test-specific SLA compliance.

Sample flow percentage

Stage count ÷ Registered count × 100

Stage counts represent milestones and must not be added together.

Pending result

Registered tests with no result-entry timestamp

The report records 7,052 pending results. Do not infer additional unrecorded statuses.

Awaiting approval

Result entered, with no approval timestamp

The report records 348 tests awaiting approval. Status groups can overlap.

Daily processing total

Source-reported daily total

Received, batched and transferred activities can overlap. The total is preserved rather than recalculated as their sum.

Defined SLA (LIS)

Configured LIS time limit, displayed as recorded

Mixed units and missing definitions prevent reliable breach calculations. Values such as “4” remain unchanged.

Daily rejections

Count of rejection events for the selected date

Daily rows total 234 rejections for the complete report period. Only available rejection-reason rows are included.
