Targets & Alerts Formulas

Period target

Sum of monthly target × selected days in that month ÷ calendar days in that month. Rounded once to the nearest rupee. An unset monthly target makes the period target unavailable.

Shortfall and excess

Shortfall = max(Target − Net Revenue, 0). Excess = max(Net Revenue − Target, 0). Targets cover the full PRO period; actuals retain the Financial Overview filters from the latest matching snapshot.

Status

Below target when revenue is lower; Above target when higher; Target met when equal. No target and Actual unavailable indicate missing inputs.

Shared target source

Saved monthly targets are used directly by Financial Overview. Values are stored in this browser, not a live shared database. Actuals are a snapshot of the latest Financial Overview selection and must match the requested dates and branch scope.
