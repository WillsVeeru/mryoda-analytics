Mr Yoda B2C Formulas & Metric Definitions

Realised revenue

Realised revenue of MR YODA bills in LIS, attributed to the channel that generated each order in the Mr Yoda app. Rounded headline values and table values retain their source precision.

Average bill and revenue share

Average bill = revenue ÷ bills, rounded to the nearest rupee. Share = row revenue ÷ total B2C revenue × 100, rounded to one decimal place. Rounded shares need not add to exactly 100%.

Booking and referral sources

Booking channel is where the order was placed. Admin desk / call centre covers walk-ins, phone and CRM bookings entered by staff. Referral is PRO when the order carries a PRO; otherwise Doctor for a real referring doctor, excluding SELF and dummy doctors; otherwise Self / direct. No Mr Yoda order remains a separate group.

Home collection and online booking

Home collection means the order has a home address, including phlebotomist-assigned orders. Online booking combines mobile app, mobile website and desktop website revenue. Home and centre columns partition revenue; PRO, doctor and self / direct columns partition it by referral and must not be added to the collection partition.

Booking conversion

Billed % = billed orders ÷ orders × 100, rounded to one decimal place. Orders = billed + cancelled + pending. Conversion follows order date; revenue follows bill date, so these counts can differ.

Bills with an order

96.2% is the source report’s order-match rate. The unrounded matching numerator is not supplied, so the displayed percentage is preserved.

Report coverage

The supplied report covers 18 Oct–16 Nov 2026. It displays ten of 25 PRO rows and ten of 30 daily rows; undisplayed rows are unavailable. Ad campaign data is empty. Full-period summaries remain separate from partial row lists. Dates can filter available daily rows; other summaries require the complete report period. Search, charts and exports operate on the available rows across pagination.
