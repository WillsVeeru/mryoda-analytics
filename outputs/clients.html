Clients Formulas & Metric Definitions

Client performance and activity

Client revenue

Revenue attributed to the selected client or client group. Negative adjustments and zero-value tests are preserved.

Active clients, bills and patients

With no client-specific filters, KPIs use the client-group totals. Client search, city or PRO filters use matching available client rows. Client type filters use the corresponding group totals.

Category and test breakdowns

Tests are test volumes, not unique bills. A bill may include several categories, so category bills must not be added to infer unique client bills.

New and dormant clients

New clients use the onboarding date and activity since onboarding. Dormant clients billed in the preceding 90 days with no billing in the selected period. Missing prior-bill amounts remain unavailable.

Available records

Charts and exports
